## 1. Intent, and the why

Anthony named the lane once, on 2026-09-05 (claim 741bd219): "Evolve the Sovereign Stack from where it is to where it needs to be. That's the lane. Tier 33 is the north star." His direction that night had five parts: a trust boundary (seats on the Studio need no tokens), house memory that every seat reads and writes directly with corrections alongside, a tools rethink, monitoring where every signal gets an owner and an end state, and probes held to the same bar as builds. Measured tonight: the trust boundary and the tools cut landed (98 to 53 tools, the seat socket live since 09-06); the ring-by-verb second phase never landed; monitoring got a ledger and a pacemaker but counters still reconcile by hand and two signal tools sit unclassified on the connector; house memory is bounded by the landing path law (pol_20260930) but its Tier 2 seldom fires because bridge handoffs carry no receipts. Each of those three unfinished parts has a line in this document (section 6 D6, section 3, section 8 item 9) so none of them rots without a status.

The thesis he approved on 2026-06-12 still governs (claim 3f7f6b79): make the Stack unable to lie and able to digest. The organs he has personally shaped since, The Ground (07-13, "give HQ this gift") and The Descent (10-07, "fix and strenthen the organ"), share one build pattern: a read-time aggregation over the record, no clock, no model, no daemon, human-gated distillation, indexing never erasing. His verbs since September are retire, fold, prune, strengthen, "make it easier not harder".

What happened to the May plan (R5): its retrieval diagnosis was right and its cheap in-stack fix shipped, as an opt-in, with the default still `newest`; four of its five risks fired; its central bet, an LLM judge upstream with confidence-gated auto-execute, was never built because the house went the other way and retired every scheduled judge (comms board 06-12, reflector and dream engine 08-08, metabolize 09-29, uncertainty 09-30), answering "what lands without him" with receipts, a second substrate and his thumb (pol_20260930). The axis it lacked, surfaces that report success on failure, became the period's main work (the write path 07-19, retrieval honesty 07-20, the handoff fail-open 08-01, a daemon halted for four months while its probe read green). It priced the dispatcher to the cent and did not price the spend-bearing surfaces that actually shipped, which emptied the balance.

So the next phase is not a new organ. It is the consolidation R2 read out of his words: the record reachable by what matters rather than by what is newest, receipts that still mean something after the file moves on, and seats that are seen (by name on every write, and the HQ seat itself watched when it goes dark). The sixteen structural debts R4 measured and the eight asks R3 found recurring across the other seats are the material; the enacted law is the bound.

## 2. Goals

G1. **Reachability.** A seat that asks the record a question gets the entries that answer it, not the newest ones that share a word. The boot's five markers are the five that matter under a stated rule, not the five newest writes above 0.9. Descriptions, discovery and the connector's classifications say what the code does. An empty search says which stores it searched.

G2. **Receipts that keep.** A `verified` receipt remains checkable after the file it names moves on; re-verify distinguishes "moved on" from "never matched"; refs that cannot survive (scratchpad paths, env-named files) are refused at write; bridge handoffs can carry receipts so the landing path's Tier 2 can fire for them.

G3. **Seats seen.** Every chronicle write carries who wrote it; per-reader queues key on a seat identity the socket already stamps; the HQ panel's own silence is detected inside the console and recorded durably; one counter per question (the Nape fix in flight tonight is the first instance).

G4. **A repo that builds safely.** A test cannot write into the live record; served code and HEAD are compared, not assumed; merged worktrees and branches are pruned; self-descriptions match the code; CI tests the dependency set the services run; unknown arguments to a write tool are refused, not dropped.

G5. **The seats' recurring asks answered or bounded.** Each of R3's eight asks gets a decision in this document: built in-phase (asks 2, 4, 5, 7), bounded with a reason (ask 6, the Ring-2 queue; the comms-write half of ask 1; the one-screen form in ask 3), or named as Anthony's with its gate shape (ask 8; the letter path in ask 1; the re-tap in ask 3; the held signal tools).
